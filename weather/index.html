Weather desk

Official National Weather Service data side by side with a global model forecast, for the places you pick.

    Blue is Open-Meteo, which blends global weather models and covers any location. Orange is the National Weather Service, the official US forecast, with alerts and the nearest station's latest measurement. NWS data exists for US locations only. When the two sources disagree by 5 degrees or 30 points of rain chance, the day is flagged. Forecasts can be wrong, so check official alerts before making safety decisions.
